How It Works

Our
Process

A disciplined four-phase process that eliminates surprises and keeps every stakeholder informed from day one to ribbon-cutting.

01
Discovery & Scoping

Site assessment, feasibility review, and a detailed scope document. We identify every variable before we price a single nail.

02
Design & Permitting

In-house coordination with architects and engineers. We manage the permit process so you don't have to chase bureaucrats.

03
Build & Oversight

Weekly site reports, milestone sign-offs, and a dedicated superintendent on-site every day the crew is working.

04
Handover & Warranty

Punch-list walkthrough, full documentation package, and a 2-year craftsmanship warranty on all Ironclad work.
